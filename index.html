01 / About

Research that works
in the real world.

I study how learning systems can make better decisions when data is limited or uncertain. My work spans offline and preference based reinforcement learning, model based planning, and world models.

I have also brought machine learning into industrial vision: estimating motion from rolling shutter cameras and building stereo depth models for precision parts. Across research and engineering, I care about careful evaluation, efficient training, and methods that hold up beyond a benchmark.

02 / Selected research

Selected research

Recent work across sequential decision making and visual sensing.

01Offline RL · ICML 2025

Reflect-then-Plan

An uncertainty aware approach to offline model based planning. The method improved performance by approximately 12% over previous state of the art methods in the reported experiments.

Read paper ↗
02Preference based RL · Ongoing

Planning from preferences

A model based approach to learning from preferences, with a sublinear regret bound for its training objective. In reported experiments, it gained roughly 15% in sample efficiency and 10% in success rate over model free baselines.

Read paper ↗
03Computer vision · ECCV 2026

Seeing motion through a rolling shutter

An optimization based method for estimating the velocity and spin of fast moving spherical objects. It reduced mean absolute error by approximately 15% compared with commercially available methods in the reported evaluation.
